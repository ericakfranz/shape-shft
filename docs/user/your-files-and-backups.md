# Your files, backups and updates

ShapeShft keeps your information in files on your own device. They do not depend on an AI's memory or a chat history, so you can start new chats, change AI providers, replace your computer or update ShapeShft without starting over.

## The three career files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `career-profile.md` | Your professional facts, career preferences, story bank, and things you do not want claimed |
| `job-library.md` | Job opportunities, with the full posting text, notes about fit, interview stages and notes |
| `practice-journal.md` | Your practice settings, a log of practice sessions, and interview reflections |

They are plain text files in a simple format called Markdown. You can open them with any text editor (Notepad on Windows, TextEdit on a Mac). You do not need to edit them yourself. Your AI assistant updates them with you.

## Saving an update

When your AI assistant gives you an updated file:

1. Save it in your `3-My-Files` folder, replacing the old file with the same name. If the assistant gave you text in a box, open the old file, select all, paste the new text, and save.
2. Replace the copy in your AI project's files, so the next chat uses the new version.

The AI always gives you the **complete** file. You never need to combine pieces by hand. If you only got part of a file, ask: "Please give me the complete updated file."

## Other things to keep

- **Applications:** in the Workbench, use **Load > Save as a .json file** for each application you send.
- **Resume and letter PDFs** from the Workbench.
- **Practice guides** you want to keep.

## Backups

Keep a second copy of your `3-My-Files` folder somewhere else: a cloud storage service you already use, a USB drive, or an email to yourself. Update the backup when your files change, for example once a week during a job search.

## Restoring

To restore, copy your backup files back into `3-My-Files` and add them to your AI project again. That is all. Your AI assistant reads them like before.

## Starting a new chat or changing AI providers

1. In your current chat, use Starter Prompt 16. It gives you updated files and a short "where we left off" note.
2. Save the files.
3. In the new chat or the new assistant, add the files (and the ShapeShft instructions), then paste the note.

## Updating ShapeShft

When a new version of ShapeShft is released:

1. Download the new ZIP and unzip it into a new folder.
2. Copy your own files from the old `3-My-Files` folder into the new `3-My-Files` folder, replacing the blank templates. Copy any saved applications and guides you want to keep.
3. In your AI project, replace `ShapeShft-AI-Instructions.md` with the new one, and update the project instructions if `Project-Instructions.txt` changed.
4. Read the "What changed" notes on the release page. If a new version ever changes the file format, the notes will include a prompt that updates your files for you.

Your career discovery does not need to be repeated.

## Deleting your information

- Delete the files in `3-My-Files`, your saved applications and guides, and your backups.
- Remove the files from your AI project, and delete the project and chats if you want to. Each AI provider has its own settings for deleting chats and data.
- To clear the Workbench's stored copy, clear the browser's site data for the Workbench file, or load the example resume.
