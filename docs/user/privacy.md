# Privacy

## The short version

- ShapeShft has no server, no account and no tracking. Nobody behind ShapeShft can see your information.
- Your career files, applications and guides are files on your own device. You control them.
- When you use an AI assistant, you share information with that AI provider. Their privacy policy applies, not ours.
- Share only what you need, and never share passwords, ID numbers or bank details.

## Where your information is

| What | Where it is stored | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Career files (`career-profile.md`, `job-library.md`, `practice-journal.md`) | Your device, and wherever you put copies (backups, your AI project) | You, and your AI provider if you upload them |
| What you type into an AI chat | The AI provider's systems | The AI provider, under their policy |
| The Resume Workbench's current resume | Your browser's local storage, on this device only | Anyone who uses this browser profile on this device |
| Letter of recommendation added in the Workbench | Your browser's local storage, on this device only | Same as above |
| Downloaded PDFs, JSON files and guides | Your Downloads folder, or wherever you save them | You |

The ShapeShft tools (the Workbench and the Practice Guide Builder) run entirely in your browser. They load nothing from the internet and send nothing to the internet. Each tool includes a security rule (a Content Security Policy) that blocks network connections. Offline practice guides contain no code at all.

## What to share with an AI assistant

Share what helps with your career: your work history, accomplishments, skills, preferences and job postings.

Do not share:

- Government ID numbers (such as a Social Security number), passport or driver's license numbers
- Bank, card or other financial account numbers
- Passwords or security codes
- Confidential information from an employer, such as private customer data, trade secrets, or anything covered by an agreement you signed
- Private information about other people, such as coworkers' personal details
- Health or other sensitive personal details you do not want to share

If you are not sure whether something is confidential, describe it in general terms ("a large retail customer" instead of the customer's name).

## AI providers

We cannot make promises about how any AI provider stores or uses your information. Check your provider's privacy settings. Many let you turn off use of your chats for training, turn off memory, and delete chats. Read their policies before you share anything sensitive.

## Interviewers and other people

It is fine to use an interviewer's name and job title to prepare. Use only professional information that is public, such as a job title or a company page. Do not collect private details about anyone.

## Sharing and reporting problems

- Do not post your career files, applications or guides publicly.
- When you report a ShapeShft problem on GitHub, issues are public. Never include personal information. Use made-up details.
