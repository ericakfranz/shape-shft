## I do not know which file to download

On the ShapeShft GitHub page, click **Releases** (on the right side on a computer, or further down on a phone). Open the newest release. Under **Assets**, download the file whose name starts with `ShapeShft-` and ends with `.zip`. Do not download "Source code". That is for developers.

## I cannot open the downloaded files

- **The ZIP file:** on Windows, right-click it and choose **Extract All**. On a Mac, double-click it. On a phone, open the Files app and tap the ZIP file. Most phones unzip it into a folder.
- **START-HERE.pdf:** if it does not open, open `START-HERE.html` in your web browser instead.
- **`.html` files** (the Workbench, the Practice Guide Builder, guides): open them with a web browser. On a computer, right-click and choose **Open with**, then your browser.
- **`.md` files** (your career files, prompts): these are plain text. Open them with a text editor such as Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac). On a phone, many file apps can show them, or you can share them to a notes app.

## I cannot find the AI feature shown in the instructions

AI apps change their menus often. Ask the assistant itself:

```
I am trying to create a project and add files and instructions to it. Where do I find that in this app? I am using A PHONE / A COMPUTER.
```

If your plan does not have projects, you can still use ShapeShft: attach `ShapeShft-AI-Instructions.md` and your career files to a normal chat and say "Please follow these ShapeShft instructions."

## My AI assistant does not remember my career information

ShapeShft does not rely on AI memory. Your information lives in your career files.

- Check that your newest `career-profile.md`, `job-library.md` and `practice-journal.md` are in your AI project's files, or attached to the chat.
- Check that you started the chat inside the ShapeShft project.
- Type `ShapeShft check`. The assistant should list the files it can see.
- If you updated a file but did not replace the copy in the project, the assistant still sees the old one.

## I do not know how to describe my accomplishments

That is normal, and it is what Career Discovery is for. You do not need to know the "right" words. Tell the assistant what you did on a normal day, and let it ask questions.

```
I find it hard to describe my accomplishments. Please ask me simple, specific questions about my work, one or two at a time, to help me remember what I did well.
```

## I do not have measurable results

Many good accomplishments have no numbers. A clear description of what changed works: what was going wrong, what you did, and what was better afterward. If you are unsure about a number, a careful estimate you are comfortable explaining ("about 30", "most weeks") can work, and ShapeShft marks it as an estimate.

```
I don't have numbers for my results. Help me describe what changed because of my work, and ask whether any honest estimates are possible.
```

## The AI is including experience I do not have

Stop and correct it. You are the authority on your experience.

```
The resume says SOMETHING I DID NOT DO. That is not true. Please remove it, add it to the "Do not claim" list in my Career Profile, and check the rest of the resume for anything else I have not confirmed. Then send the complete JSON again.
```

The Workbench also shows a red FIX item when something from your "do not claim" list appears. Always read every line before you send a resume.

## The Workbench will not load my application

The Load tab shows a message. The usual causes:

- **Extra text was copied.** Copy only the code block, using its copy button if there is one. The text should start with `{` and end with `}`.
- **The JSON was cut off.** Long replies sometimes stop early. If the end of the text is not `}`, the reply was cut off.
- **A small formatting error,** such as a missing comma.

```
The Resume Workbench says: PASTE THE MESSAGE. Please send the complete JSON again, corrected, in one code block.
```

## My resume is too long

The fit meter above the sheet shows how full the page is.

- Delete the weakest bullet from an older role (hover over it and click the x).
- Ask your AI assistant: "Please shorten my resume to one page. Keep the most relevant evidence for this job, and return the complete JSON."
- Two pages is normal for long careers. Ask what is right for your situation.

## I cannot export my resume

- You must click **Approve** first. The download button is disabled until you do.
- Check your browser's Downloads folder. Some browsers ask where to save, or block downloads from local files the first time. Allow them.
- If the Workbench says the PDF library did not load, the file may be damaged. Download ShapeShft again and use the new `ShapeShft-Resume-Workbench.html`.
- Try another browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

## I do not know how to begin talking out loud

Start very small. Pick one sentence starter and say it, then say whatever comes next, even if it is short.

- "On a normal day at work, I would..."
- "The first thing I did when I got to work was..."
- "Something that annoyed me at work was..."

Describe something you can picture: the room, the tools, the first hour of the day. It is fine if it feels strange at first. Ask your coach for Guided support:

```
I find it hard to start talking out loud. Please give me a very short, very easy practice session with lots of sentence starters.
```

## I keep losing my train of thought

That is normal, and it is a skill you can practice. When it happens:

- Pause. You do not need to apologize.
- Say: "The main point I'm trying to make is..."
- Start the story again from a different point.
- Ask yourself a question out loud: "Why did that matter?"

Every ShapeShft practice guide ends with an "If you get stuck" section with these tools.

## I cannot remember specific professional examples

Memories often come back when you describe details. Try describing a specific place, a busy day, a difficult customer, or a change at work, and let your coach ask follow-up questions. Old performance reviews, emails you sent, or photos of your workplace can help. Things you remember go into your Career Profile, so you do not have to remember them twice.

## I feel like I am repeating myself during practice

Repeating yourself is part of learning. It means you are finding the parts that matter. To add variety, ask your coach for a new angle:

```
I keep telling this story the same way. Give me three different angles to explore it from, and a different listener to imagine.
```

## I cannot open my offline coaching document

- Make sure you opened the downloaded `.html` file, not the guide text.
- On a phone, a file preview may show the guide oddly. Try opening it in a web browser instead. See [Offline practice guides](practice-guides.md).
- Email the file to yourself, or put it in your cloud storage, then open it on your phone from there.
- As a last resort, open the guide on a computer and print it.

## I have reached my AI usage limit

Free plans limit how many messages you can send in a few hours.

- Before you hit the limit, ask for your updated files (Starter Prompt 16).
- Wait for the limit to reset. The app usually says when.
- Meanwhile, practice with an offline guide. Spoken practice does not need the AI.
- You can continue in a different AI assistant with your files.

## I want to start a new conversation without losing my progress

Use Starter Prompt 16 in your current chat. Save the files it gives you, then start the new chat with those files and the "where we left off" note.

## I downloaded a newer version of ShapeShft

Copy your own files from the old `3-My-Files` folder into the new one, and replace `ShapeShft-AI-Instructions.md` in your AI project. Details: [Your files, backups and updates](your-files-and-backups.md#updating-shapeshft).

## Reporting a problem

Report problems on the ShapeShft GitHub page under **Issues**. **Never include personal information** in an issue: no resumes, career files, job applications, names, contact details or employer details. Issues are public. Describe the problem with made-up details instead.
