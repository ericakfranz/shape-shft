### Full posting text

Internal posting: Service Manager

Kestrel Mechanical is creating a Service Manager position to lead our service department as we grow.

The Service Manager will:
- Lead and support our team of 9 service technicians and 3 apprentices
- Work with the service coordinator to schedule and dispatch calls, putting emergencies first
- Review service tickets for quality and follow up on callbacks
- Train and mentor technicians and apprentices, and plan ongoing training
- Handle escalated customer concerns
- Track service department results and report to the owners monthly
- Provide occasional field support on complex calls
- Help with some evening and weekend on-call coordination

We are looking for:
- 5+ years of residential HVAC service experience
- Journeyman license required; EPA 608 Universal required
- Experience training or leading other technicians
- Strong customer communication
- Comfortable with ServiceTitan
- Interest in growing into a leadership role

Interested employees should send a resume and a short letter to Gail by November 30.
