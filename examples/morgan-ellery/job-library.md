### Full posting text

Operations Manager - Distribution Center
Larkspur Outdoor Supply - Fairmont Junction, OH (on-site)
$62,000 - $70,000 per year

About us
Larkspur Outdoor Supply sells camping, hiking and paddling gear online and through 12 retail stores. Our Fairmont Junction distribution center opened in 2023 and ships to stores and directly to customers.

About the role
The Operations Manager leads day-to-day operations in our distribution center. You will lead two shift supervisors and a team of about 45 associates across receiving, inventory, picking, packing and shipping. You will improve our processes as we grow, and you will have the chance to grow into a site leadership role.

What you will do
- Lead daily operations to meet shipping deadlines for stores and online orders
- Own inventory accuracy, including cycle count programs and root cause investigation
- Build and adjust staffing schedules to match volume
- Train and develop shift supervisors and associates
- Lead safety programs and make sure we follow OSHA requirements
- Track labor costs and productivity, and help prepare the annual labor budget
- Find and lead continuous improvement projects
- Work with purchasing, customer service and store teams

What you bring
- 5+ years of experience in a warehouse or distribution center
- 2+ years supervising hourly teams
- Experience with a warehouse management system (WMS)
- Strong record of improving inventory accuracy
- Experience training and coaching others
- Working knowledge of OSHA requirements; forklift experience a plus
- Comfortable with Excel or Google Sheets

Nice to have
- Experience with Lean or other continuous improvement methods
- Experience managing a labor budget
- Bachelor's degree in supply chain, business or a related field

Schedule
Monday to Friday, day shift. Rotating Saturday coverage during peak season (October to December). Occasionally on call for after-hours issues.

Benefits
Health, dental and vision insurance, 401(k) with company match, paid time off, employee discount.
