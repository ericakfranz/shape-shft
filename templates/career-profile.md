This file is the main record of your professional experience. Your AI assistant reads it and updates it with you. You do not need to edit it by hand, but you can. Keep the newest copy in your ShapeShft folder. Resumes are made from this file, so a resume is never the source of your facts.

How to read the tags:

- `[confirmed]` You said this is true.
- `[estimate]` You gave this as a careful estimate, and you are comfortable using it as one.
- `[to clarify]` Something is missing or unclear. Do not use it in an application yet.
- `[unknown]` Nobody knows yet. That does not mean it did not happen.
- `[AI suggestion]` The AI's idea or interpretation. It is not a fact until you confirm it.
