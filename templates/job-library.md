This file keeps the job opportunities you are applying to, considering, or using for practice. Postings often disappear, so each one keeps the full original text. Your AI assistant updates this file with you.

How to read the labels:

- `[from posting]` Written in the job posting.
- `[verified]` Checked in a reliable source. The source is named.
- `[interviewer said]` Said by someone at the company.
- `[my observation]` Something you noticed.
- `[my inference]` Something you concluded. It may be wrong.
- `[AI note]` The AI's interpretation. It is not a fact about the employer.
- `[unknown]` Not known yet.

Status values: `considering`, `applied`, `interviewing`, `offer`, `accepted`, `declined`, `rejected`, `closed`, `practice only`.
