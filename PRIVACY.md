# ShapeShft privacy

- ShapeShft has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The maintainers cannot see anything you do with it.
- Your career files, applications and practice guides are files on your own device.
- The Resume Workbench and the Practice Guide Builder run in your browser and make no network requests. The Workbench keeps your current resume in your browser's local storage on that device only.
- When you use an AI assistant, you share information with that AI provider, and their privacy policy applies. ShapeShft cannot make promises about any provider.
- Do not share passwords, government ID numbers, bank or card numbers, confidential employer information, or private information about other people.
- Issues on GitHub are public. Never include personal information in an issue.

The full explanation, including where each kind of information is stored and how to delete it, is in [docs/user/privacy.md](docs/user/privacy.md). It is also in the `Help` folder of every release.
