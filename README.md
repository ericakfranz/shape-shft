# ShapeShft

A free, open-source toolkit for career development. It helps you make truthful, tailored resumes, practice talking about your work out loud, and prepare for interviews, using your own AI assistant.

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)** · **[START HERE](START-HERE.md)**

---

## What ShapeShft does

ShapeShft helps you:

- **Write down your experience.** A guided conversation helps you remember what you did at work, including things you may not think are important.
- **Apply for jobs truthfully.** It checks each job posting against what matters to you, asks about experience you may have left out, and writes a tailored resume and cover letter. You edit and download them as PDFs.
- **Practice talking about your work.** Short daily exercises where you talk out loud to yourself about real experiences, using real jobs as the context. You can take a practice guide on a walk.
- **Prepare for interviews.** Preparation for each interviewer, questions to ask the employer, and reflection afterward.
- **Make informed decisions.** Compare opportunities against your own priorities, not only against whether you might get an offer.

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants to describe their work clearly: people looking for a new job, a promotion, a career change or a raise, and people returning to work. It is written for every kind of work, including trades, retail, care work, office work, technical work and management. You do not need to have used AI before, and you do not need numbers for every accomplishment.

## Two parts, used together or separately

| Resume Builder | Conversation Coach |
|---|---|
| Career Discovery, job analysis, notes on fit with your priorities, tailored resumes and cover letters, and the Resume Workbench for editing and PDF download | Daily spoken practice, a story bank of your experiences, job-specific practice, interview preparation, questions to ask, reflection, and offline practice guides |

Both use the same three files about you, so you only tell your story once.

## How it works

1. Download and unzip ShapeShft.
2. Set up a project in your AI assistant with the ShapeShft instructions (about 10 minutes, once).
3. Talk with your assistant to build your Career Profile.
4. Paste a job posting to get a tailored application. Edit and download it in the Resume Workbench, which runs in your web browser.
5. Practice talking out loud with sessions from the Conversation Coach, online or with an offline guide.
6. Save your updated files on your device. Keep going.

## Speaking out loud is the practice

The Conversation Coach is not a list of answers to memorize. Its main exercise is talking out loud to yourself about your real experience until you can explain it comfortably in your own words, however a conversation goes.

## Honest by design

- It never invents experience, numbers or credentials. You are the authority on your own career.
- It keeps confirmed facts separate from estimates and AI suggestions.
- It does not try to trick applicant tracking systems (ATS), and it does not promise interviews, jobs or salaries.
- It is not built for sending large numbers of applications.

## What you need

- A computer, tablet or phone with a web browser. A computer is easiest for editing resumes.
- An AI assistant account, such as Claude or ChatGPT. Free plans are enough to start.
- No coding, no installation, no terminal.

## Costs

ShapeShft is free. AI assistants have free plans that can run ShapeShft. Paid plans give you more messages per day and some conveniences. They are optional. See [Choosing an AI assistant](docs/user/choosing-an-ai-assistant.md).

## Privacy

ShapeShft has no server, no account and no tracking. Your files stay on your device. The tools run in your browser and connect to nothing. When you use an AI assistant, you share information with that provider, and their privacy policy applies. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md).
